# 📊 HR Analytics & Performance Dashboard

## 📝 Overview
A comprehensive Power BI project transforming raw HR data (30+ columns) into key workforce insights, tracking performance, and analyzing the root causes of employee attrition.

* **Domain:** Human Resources
* **Target:** HR Managers & Talent Development Teams

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Data Prep & ETL:** Excel & Power Query
* **Data Modeling & Analysis:** Power BI (DAX)
* **Visualization:** Power BI (Interactive 3-page Dashboard)

---

## ✨ Key Highlights
* **🔄 Integration & Data Pipeline:** Executed an end-to-end ETL process using Power Query to clean, transform, and structure 30+ raw columns into an optimized relational data model.
* **📐 Advanced Analytics:** Developed a custom, unweighted **Employee Experience Index (EXI)** using statistical methods to combine complex satisfaction and environment indicators without bias.
* **📈 Multi-Page Interactive Reporting:**
  * **Page 1 - Overview:** Department & Job-level heat maps for quick high-level tracking.
  * **Page 2 - Attrition Analysis:** Uncovered correlations between distance-from-work, compensation, and turnover rates to drive policy improvements.
  * **Page 3 - Performance & Talent:** Dynamic grid filtering top EXI & high-performance scorers to streamline promotion decisions.

---

## 🎯 Challenge & Solution
* 🔴 **Challenge:** High column count and multi-variable complexity making overall employee evaluation difficult.
* 🟢 **Solution:** Engineered the composite **EXI metric** to aggregate satisfaction metrics into a single, unbiased baseline for clear executive decision-making.
